About

Hi, I'm Anushka — a dancer and choreographer who believes movement is the purest form of expression. I started dancing at the age of six and never looked back.

Over the years I've had the privilege of performing on some incredible stages and collaborating with artists who push me to grow every single day. My style blends classical training with contemporary flair — always evolving, always authentic.

When I'm not in the studio I'm teaching workshops and helping others discover the joy of movement. Follow along for behind-the-scenes content, routines, and a whole lot of good vibes.
